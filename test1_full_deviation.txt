DEVIATION REPORT

Deviation No.: DEV-2026-041
Title: Drying temperature excursion in Fluid Bed Dryer FBD-02
Product: Atorvastatin Calcium API
Batch No.: AT-26-0412
Process Stage: Final drying
Equipment ID: FBD-02 (Fluid Bed Dryer)
Area / Location: Production Block B, Drying Room 2
Department: API Manufacturing
Date of Occurrence: 28-Oct-2026 (approx. 02:15 to 06:40)
Date Detected: 28-Oct-2026, 07:00
Detected By: Ravi Kumar, Production Operator (during shift handover)
Classification (initial): Major

Description:
During the final drying of batch AT-26-0412, the inlet air temperature on FBD-02 rose to 78 C against the validated limit of 60 C (+/- 5 C). The excursion lasted about 4 hours 25 minutes during the night shift. No high-temperature alarm was triggered on the control panel. The excursion was noticed by the incoming morning shift operator when reviewing the trend chart at handover.

Observations at the time of discovery:
- Temperature sensor TT-204 on FBD-02 was reading normally on the local display.
- The alarm set point on the control panel was found at 90 C instead of 65 C.
- The night shift operator on duty had joined the company three weeks earlier.
- The batch record for the drying step was filled in at 07:30, after the excursion was found.

Immediate actions taken:
1. Dryer stopped and batch AT-26-0412 placed on QA hold.
2. Samples sent to QC for assay, related substances and loss on drying.
3. Production Head and QA Head informed at 07:20.

Impact assessment (preliminary):
Possible degradation of the API and higher related substances. Residual solvent levels may also be affected. Other batches dried in FBD-02 during the last 30 days are to be reviewed.
